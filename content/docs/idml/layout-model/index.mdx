**IDML's layout model is the set of containers that decides where content lands: spreads, the pages in them, and the page items placed on them.**

**In short:** An IDML document is a sequence of **spreads**, one part per spread. A spread
holds its **pages** and its **page items** side by side: frames, shapes and groups are
children of the `<Spread>`, positioned in the spread's coordinate space, and each page is
a rectangle placed in that same space. Which page an item is on follows from geometry.
**Master spreads** hold items that repeat on every page that applies them, and a page can
**override** single master items. This chapter walks that nesting from the outside in.

## The containers

- **Document.** `designmap.xml` lists the spreads and master spreads in order, along with
  the document-wide layers, sections and preferences.
- **Spread.** One `Spreads/Spread_*.xml` part per spread. The spread's space has its origin
  on the spine.
- **Page.** A `<Page>` inside the spread: a rectangle (`GeometricBounds`) placed by its own
  `ItemTransform`, with a master, margins, columns and guides.
- **Page item.** Every visible thing: `TextFrame`, `Rectangle`, `Oval`, `GraphicLine`,
  `Polygon`, `Group`. Items are siblings of the pages; their order in the spread is their
  stacking order.
- **Master spread.** The same structure, in `MasterSpreads/MasterSpread_*.xml`, referenced
  from pages by `AppliedMaster`.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the layout model in IDML?**
The nesting of containers that positions content: a document of spreads, each spread
holding pages and page items, with master spreads supplying repeated items to the pages
that apply them.

**Are page items children of pages?**
No. Page items are children of the `<Spread>`, next to the `<Page>` elements, and they are
positioned in spread coordinates. The page an item belongs to is the one its geometry falls
on.

**How does a master spread relate to a body page?**
A body page names a master in `AppliedMaster`. The master page at the same position in the
master spread supplies its items to the body page, behind the page's own items, unless the
page has overridden them.
