**A master spread is a spread whose items appear on every body page that applies it.**

**In short:** Running heads, folios, background panels and frame templates are drawn once,
on a master spread, in `MasterSpreads/MasterSpread_*.xml`. A body `<Page>` names a master
in `AppliedMaster`, and the master page at the same position supplies its items to that
page, behind the page's own items. The items are not copied into the body spread's XML.
A page can override single master items. The details are in the
[Master spreads & overrides](/docs/idml/master-spreads-overrides) chapter.

## A master spread is a spread

A `<MasterSpread>` has the same structure as a `<Spread>`: `<Page>` children placed by
their `ItemTransform`, followed by page items in spread coordinates. It adds a name,
written three ways (`NamePrefix` such as `A`, `BaseName` such as `Master`, and the `Name`
they make, `A-Master`), and it is listed in `designmap.xml` before the body spreads.

A body page links to it by the master's `Self` id: `AppliedMaster="umaster"`. A facing
master has a left and a right page; the first page of a body spread takes its items from
the master's first page, the second from the second. A master page can itself apply
another master (*based on* a master); its `AppliedMaster` names the parent, or `n` for none.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is a master spread in IDML?**
A spread of repeating items in a `MasterSpreads/` part. Every body page that names it in
`AppliedMaster` shows its items behind the page's own content.

**How does a body page apply a master?**
With `AppliedMaster` set to the master spread's `Self` id. `n` means no master.

**Are master items copied into the body spread?**
No. They stay in the master part. A body spread only holds the items drawn on its own
pages, plus any master items the page has overridden and so owns.
