**A `Spread` is the coordinate space of one opening; each `Page` is a rectangle placed in that space by its own `ItemTransform`.**

**In short:** A `Spreads/Spread_*.xml` part holds one `<Spread>`. Its children are its
`<Page>` elements followed by its page items. The spread's space has x = 0 on the spine.
Each page has its own `GeometricBounds` and an `ItemTransform` that places it in the
spread, which is how the two pages of a facing spread end up side by side. InDesign always
writes `PageCount`, `BindingLocation` and `ItemTransform` on a spread, and it needs them:
without the first two it can open a one-page spread as a facing pair with an empty page.

## The `Spread` element

<AttrTable element="Spread" />

- **`PageCount`** is the number of `<Page>` children. **`BindingLocation`** is the index of
  the spine among them: `0` for a single page (the spine is at its left edge), `1` for a
  facing pair. InDesign writes both on every spread and reads them: given a one-page spread
  without them, it opened the spread as a facing pair and added an empty page.
- **`ItemTransform`** places the spread on the pasteboard. InDesign uses its translation to
  stack spreads one below the other; it does not move items relative to their pages. A
  rotation in it turns the whole spread.
- **`ShowMasterItems`** decides whether the pages of this spread show their master's items.
  It is a spread attribute: InDesign writes it on `<Spread>` and `<MasterSpread>` and
  ignores it on `<Page>`. See [master items and stamping](/docs/idml/master-spreads-overrides/master-items-and-stamping).
- **`AllowPageShuffle`** and **`FlattenerOverride`** are editing and output settings that do
  not change what the spread looks like.

> **In Paged:** a spread's rotation and scale are applied when rendering; its translation
> cancels against the pages. `PageCount` and `BindingLocation` are not read on import (the
> pages are counted) and are always written on export.

## The `Page` element

<AttrTable element="Page" />

- **`GeometricBounds`** is the page rectangle in the page's own space, `top left bottom
  right`. It is the page's size.
- **`ItemTransform`** maps those bounds into the spread. InDesign's own files place the left
  page of an A4 spread at `1 0 0 1 -595.276 -420.945` and the right page at
  `1 0 0 1 0 -420.945`: the spine at x = 0, the pages centred vertically on y = 0. Always
  write it; InDesign does not read a missing one as the identity.
- **`AppliedMaster`** is the `Self` of the master spread applied to the page, or `n` for
  none. **`MasterPageTransform`** maps the master page onto this page; usually the identity.
- **`OverrideList`** names master items the page has overridden; see
  [override resolution](/docs/idml/master-spreads-overrides/override-resolution).
- **`Name`** is the page label as InDesign displays it: `1`, `iii`, `A-3`. It reflects the
  section numbering; see [sections, numbering & variables](/docs/idml/sections-numbering-variables).

## What a page contains

A `<Page>` has no page items. It can hold:

- **`MarginPreference`**: the margins and the column grid (`ColumnCount`, `ColumnGutter`)
  that guide layout on this page. They do not clip anything.
- **`Guide`**: ruler guides placed on this page. `Guide` elements also appear directly
  under the `<Spread>`.
- **`Properties`** with a `Label`: key/value metadata attached to the page by scripts and
  plugins.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the difference between a spread and a page?**
A `<Spread>` is a coordinate space holding the pages and items of one opening. A `<Page>`
is a rectangle placed in that space by its `ItemTransform`. Page items are children of the
spread, not of the page.

**Why does a page need both GeometricBounds and ItemTransform?**
The bounds give the page's size in its own space; the transform puts it in the spread. On
a facing spread the transforms set the pages side by side, with the left page at negative x.

**Which attributes must a writer put on a Spread?**
At least `Self`, `PageCount`, `BindingLocation` and `ItemTransform`, as InDesign does.
Without `PageCount` and `BindingLocation`, InDesign may treat a single page as one half of
a facing pair and add an empty page.

**Where does ShowMasterItems go?**
On the `<Spread>`. InDesign ignores it on a `<Page>`.
