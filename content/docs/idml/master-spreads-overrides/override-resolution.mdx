**A body page's `OverrideList` names the master items it has taken over; those items are no longer shown from the master.**

**In short:** An author usually wants most of a master and a different version of one item:
a chapter title where the master has a generic head. Overriding a master item in InDesign
copies it into the body spread as an ordinary, editable page item, and records the master
item's `Self` id in the page's `OverrideList`. A reader resolves a page by showing every
master item *except* the ones listed there, and then the page's own items, the copy among
them. Listing an id with no copy deletes that master item from the page.

## What `OverrideList` records

`OverrideList` is an attribute of the body `<Page>`: a space-separated list of `Self` ids of
items on that page's master. Resolution has two sides that must agree:

1. The listed master items are **left out** of the page.
2. The page's **own copy** paints as an ordinary item of the body spread, in its own position
   in the stacking order and on its own layer.

The copy is not tied to the master item by any attribute in the files surveyed for this
reference: the copy has its own `Self` id, and the only link is the master item's id in the
page's list. Because the list alone decides, an id with no copy on the page is a clean
**deletion** of that master item from that page.

The list is per page. On a facing spread, overriding the left folio touches only the left
page's list; the right page still shows its master folio.

## Attributes a reader can ignore

- **`AllowOverrides`** on a master item says whether InDesign's user may override it.
  InDesign writes ids into `OverrideList` only for items that allow it, so a reader needs
  only the list.
- **`OverriddenPageItemProps`** appears on page items and on `<MasterSpread>`. It is
  InDesign's bookkeeping of which properties of an item were edited. The edited values are
  already on the copy, so the result does not depend on it.

> **In Paged:** master items named in a page's `OverrideList` are left out when the master is
> placed; `AllowOverrides` and `OverriddenPageItemProps` are not read.

## Frequently asked questions

**What does the OverrideList attribute do?**
It lists, on a body `<Page>`, the `Self` ids of master items the page has taken over. Those
items are not shown from the master on that page.

**Where does the overridden copy live?**
In the body spread, as an ordinary page item with its own `Self` id. It paints like any
other item of the page.

**What happens if I list a master item but supply no copy?**
The master item disappears from that page. The list alone decides what is left out.

**Does a master item need AllowOverrides for an override to work?**
Not for a reader. `AllowOverrides` governs what InDesign lets a user do; the result is
decided by the page's `OverrideList`.

**Can a page override only some properties of a master item?**
The override copies the whole item into the page, and the edited properties are on that
copy. `OverriddenPageItemProps` records which properties were edited, but the copy already
holds the result.
