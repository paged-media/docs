**A `Section` says where page numbering restarts and how its numbers look; each page's label is the result.**

**In short:** A `Section` element in `designmap.xml` starts at a page (`PageStart`) and
covers `Length` pages. It either continues the previous section's count or restarts at
`PageNumberStart`, in the style named by a typed `<Properties><PageNumberStyle>` child
(`Arabic`, `LowerRoman`, `UpperLetters`, …). It can add a `SectionPrefix` to its
numbers and defines the text of its section marker in `Marker`. Every document has at
least one section. Page-number markers in stories (`<?ACE 18?>`) print the number the
section gives the page; `<?ACE 19?>` prints the section's marker.

## Where sections go

Sections are direct children of `Document` in the design map, after the `idPkg:Spread`
references and before the stories. Every InDesign-exported document in the census (271
of them) carries at least one: InDesign writes a section on the first page even when the
numbering never restarts. Each section covers its pages up to the start of the next.

## The `Section` element

- `PageStart` names the `Self` of the page where the section begins, and `Length` is the
  number of pages it covers. InDesign writes `Length` on every section.
- `ContinueNumbering="true"` carries the count on from the previous section;
  `ContinueNumbering="false"` restarts at `PageNumberStart`.
- `SectionPrefix` is text such as "A-" that can be shown with the page numbers;
  `IncludeSectionPrefix` decides whether it is.
- `Marker` is the section marker: the text a `<?ACE 19?>` marker prints on the pages of
  this section, such as a chapter title.
- `Name` is the section's name in InDesign's interface, usually empty.

<AttrTable element="Section" />

These rules were measured in InDesign 20.0.1:

- **Write `ContinueNumbering="false"` before `PageNumberStart`.** InDesign applies the
  start number only once it has read the restart; with the attributes the other way
  round, it keeps the section and its marker but numbers straight through.
- **An absent `ContinueNumbering` means continue.** A restart must say `"false"`.

## The numbering style

The style is a typed property, not an attribute:
`<Properties><PageNumberStyle type="enumeration">LowerRoman</PageNumberStyle></Properties>`.
It is a property because its value can be either one of the built-in styles or a
reference to a custom numbering list. The common built-in values are:

| `PageNumberStyle` | Numbers as |
|---|---|
| `Arabic` | 1, 2, 3 |
| `UpperRoman` | I, II, III |
| `LowerRoman` | i, ii, iii |
| `UpperLetters` | A, B, C |
| `LowerLetters` | a, b, c |

A `PageNumberStyle` attribute on `Section` appears only in generated files; InDesign
writes the property.

## How pages, markers and chapters use it

- **The page's label.** Every `Page` carries a `Name`, the label InDesign shows for it,
  with the section's style applied. In the example the two pages are named `ii` and `1`.
- **Page-number markers.** `<?ACE 18?>` inside a story's `Content` prints the current
  page's number, or with `PageNumberType="NextPageNumber"` or
  `"PreviousPageNumber"` on its range, the number of the page the story continues on or
  came from. All of them use the numbering of the page they refer to. See
  [text variables](/docs/idml/sections-numbering-variables/text-variables) for the full
  marker table.
- **The section marker.** `<?ACE 19?>` prints the `Marker` of the section the page
  belongs to.
- **The chapter number** is not part of a section. It is the document's own
  `ChapterNumber`, set in `ChapterNumberPreference` in `Resources/Preferences.xml`, and
  the `ChapterNumberType` text variable prints it.

> **In Paged:** sections are read in both spellings of the numbering style; Arabic, Roman
> and letter styles are applied, and any other style falls back to Arabic. A page's
> label comes from its `Name`; only a page without one is numbered from the sections, and
> the build reports when that happens. Scripts can add, edit and remove sections, which
> re-labels the pages, and saving writes InDesign's spelling.

## Frequently asked questions

**Where is page numbering defined in IDML?**
In `Section` elements in `designmap.xml`. Each section starts at a page, covers `Length`
pages, and either continues the count or restarts at `PageNumberStart`.

**How is the numbering style written?**
As a typed property:
`<Properties><PageNumberStyle type="enumeration">LowerRoman</PageNumberStyle></Properties>`.
The value can also name a custom numbering list, which is why it is not an attribute.

**Why does my section restart not take effect?**
Check the attribute order: InDesign applies `PageNumberStart` only when
`ContinueNumbering="false"` comes before it. An absent `ContinueNumbering` means the
numbering continues.

**What does `<?ACE 19?>` print?**
The `Marker` text of the section the page belongs to. The page number itself is
`<?ACE 18?>`.

**Is the chapter number a section setting?**
No. It belongs to the document, in `ChapterNumberPreference`, and defaults to 1.
