**A feather fades the object itself, not a copy of it: a basic feather softens every edge, a directional feather chosen sides, and a gradient feather fades the whole object along a ramp of opacity stops.**

**In short:** `FeatherSetting` softens all edges over `Width` points and is switched on by
`Mode="Standard"`. `DirectionalFeatherSetting` gives each side its own width (`LeftWidth`,
`RightWidth`, `TopWidth`, `BottomWidth`). `GradientFeatherSetting` fades the object along a
linear or radial ramp defined by `<OpacityGradientStop>` children. The last two are switched on
by `Applied="true"`. All three sit with the other effects in a `TransparencySetting` (or a
fill, stroke or content one).

## Basic feather

<AttrTable element="FeatherSetting" />

- **`Mode`** is `Standard` when the feather is on and `None` when it is off. Unlike the other
  effects, the basic feather has no `Applied` attribute.
- **`Width`** is how far in from the edge the fade reaches, in points. InDesign's default is 9.
- **`ChokeAmount`** moves the fade inward, as a percentage of the width.
- **`CornerType`** shapes the fade at corners; `Diffusion` is the default and the only value in
  the surveyed files.
- **`Noise`** adds grain to the fade.

## Directional feather

<AttrTable element="DirectionalFeatherSetting" />

Each side fades over its own width; a side with width 0 keeps a hard edge. `Angle` rotates the
directions the widths apply to. `FollowShapeMode` (`LeadingEdge` in every surveyed file)
decides how the widths follow a shape that is not a rectangle. `ChokeAmount` and `Noise` work as
for the basic feather.

## Gradient feather

<AttrTable element="GradientFeatherSetting" />

A gradient feather fades the object along a ramp, like a gradient fill whose stops are
opacities instead of colours:

- **`Type`** is `Linear` or `Radial`.
- **`GradientStart`** is the start of the ramp, an `x y` pair in the object's coordinates;
  **`Length`** its length in points; **`Angle`** its direction in degrees.
- **`HiliteAngle`** and **`HiliteLength`** are written alongside; they do not set the ramp's
  direction. On a radial feather, InDesign moves the focal point of the fade toward the
  circle's edge along `HiliteAngle`.
- The ramp is defined by **`<OpacityGradientStop>`** children, each with an `Opacity` (percent),
  a `Location` (percent along the ramp) and a `Midpoint`, as on a gradient swatch's stops.

<AttrTable element="OpacityGradientStop" />

Gradient feather settings appear on page items in about twenty of the surveyed documents,
with opacity stops in six.

> **In Paged:** all three feathers are drawn. The basic feather reads `CornerType`
> `Diffusion`, `Rounded` and `Sharp`; the gradient feather takes its direction from `Angle`, not
> from `HiliteAngle`. A radial gradient feather with no `GradientStart` of its own is placed
> where InDesign places it, measured against its PDF output. The radial focal point that
> `HiliteAngle` and `HiliteLength` move is not applied yet, and `FollowShapeMode` is read and
> saved back. <SupportBadge feature="effects-transparency.feather" /> <SupportBadge feature="effects-transparency.gradient-feather" />

## Frequently asked questions

**How is a basic feather switched on?**
With `Mode="Standard"` on `<FeatherSetting>`. It has no `Applied` attribute; `Mode="None"` is
off.

**What does `Width` mean on a feather?**
How far the fade reaches in from the object's edge, in points.

**Can a feather fade only one side?**
Yes, with `DirectionalFeatherSetting`: give that side a width and leave the others at 0.

**How are a gradient feather's opacities written?**
As `<OpacityGradientStop>` children of `<GradientFeatherSetting>`, each with `Opacity`,
`Location` and `Midpoint`.

**Is a gradient feather the same as a gradient fill to transparent?**
No. A gradient feather fades the whole object, stroke and content included, whatever its fill.
