**Six effects darken or lighten an object's edges. Their numbers are InDesign's: `Size` is the width of the soft band, `Spread` and `Choke` are percentages of it, and `Angle` says where the light comes from.**

**In short:** A drop shadow is offset by `XOffset` and `YOffset`; an inner shadow, a satin
and a bevel by an `Angle` and a `Distance`, where the angle names the direction of the light,
so the shadow falls the opposite way. `Size` is how wide the soft edge is, not a blur radius:
an outer shadow or glow has faded to about a sixth of its strength `Size / 2` beyond the
object's edge and is nearly gone at `Size`. `Spread` (outer effects) and `ChokeAmount`
(inner effects) are percentages of `Size` that harden the edge. The drop shadow is on with
`Mode="Drop"`, the others with `Applied="true"`.

## How InDesign reads the numbers

These rules were measured by having InDesign draw each effect and comparing its output with
the values in its own IDML.

- **`Size` is the width of the band.** For effects that spill outside the object (drop shadow,
  outer glow) the edge is a Gaussian falloff with a standard deviation of `Size / 2`: at
  `Size / 2` outside the outline about 16 % of the strength is left, at `Size` about 2 %.
  Effects that stay inside the object (inner shadow, inner glow) fall off with a standard
  deviation of about `0.4 × Size`. Reading `Size` as the blur radius doubles the softness.
- **`Spread` and `ChokeAmount` are percentages of `Size`.** Spread grows the shape outward
  before the falloff; choke moves the edge inward. Neither is a distance in points.
- **`Angle` names the light.** It is measured in degrees counter-clockwise from the right,
  and the shadow falls away from the light: with y pointing down the page, the offset is
  `x = −Distance × cos(Angle)` and `y = +Distance × sin(Angle)`. InDesign's default, 120°,
  puts the light at the upper left and the shadow down and to the right.
- **A drop shadow's offset is written as `XOffset` and `YOffset`.** Positive is right and down.
  Inner shadows and satins are written with `Angle` and `Distance`.
- **`UseGlobalLight="true"`** takes the angle from the document's light, `GlobalLightAngle`
  (and for a bevel `GlobalLightAltitude`) on `<TransparencyPreference>`. It is `false` in every
  surveyed file.

`Opacity` is a percentage of the effect's strength, `Noise` a percentage of grain, and
`EffectColor` names a palette colour; `n` leaves the effect's default colour.

## Drop shadow

<AttrTable element="DropShadowSetting" />

On with `Mode="Drop"`, off with `Mode="None"`. InDesign's default is a black, 75 %-opaque
`Multiply` shadow, 7 pt right and 7 pt down, `Size` 5. `KnockedOut="true"` hides the shadow
behind a semi-transparent object; `HonorOtherEffects` lets other effects shape the shadow. In
the surveyed documents, drop shadows are switched on in 10.

### What casts the shadow

A drop shadow is cast by what the object paints. InDesign follows these rules for every kind of
shape:

- **A filled shape** casts the shadow of its outline, grown by the part of a visible stroke
  that lies outside the outline. A rounded rectangle casts a rounded shadow, an ellipse an
  elliptical one.
- **A shape with no fill and a visible stroke** casts the shadow of its stroke band only.
- **A line** casts the shadow of its stroke, at the stroke's width, with its caps.
- **A text frame with no fill** casts the shadow of its text, glyph by glyph.

A shadow inside `StrokeTransparencySetting` is cast by the stroke alone; one inside
`ContentTransparencySetting` by the content alone.

> **In Paged:** drop shadows are drawn for rectangles, ovals, polygons, lines and text frames
> by these rules, matched to InDesign on test pages for lines, ovals, polygons and rectangles.
> A group's shadow is drawn for the group as a whole. A shadow at the stroke level is kept as
> the stroke's shadow; one at the content level is not drawn.

## Inner shadow

<AttrTable element="InnerShadowSetting" />

A shadow inside the object's edge, as if the object were cut out of the page. Positioned by
`Angle` and `Distance` (default 120° and 7 pt), softened by `Size`, hardened by
`ChokeAmount`. Default `Multiply`, 75 %.

> **In Paged:** inner shadows are drawn by the rules above, the light angle included.
> <SupportBadge feature="effects-transparency.inner-shadow" />

## Outer glow and inner glow

<AttrTable element="OuterGlowSetting" />

<AttrTable element="InnerGlowSetting" />

A glow is an unoffset band of colour around the outside of the edge (outer) or inside it
(inner). `Size` sets its width, `Spread` hardens it. Inner glows spell their choke `Spread`
too. `Source` on an inner glow is `EdgeSourced` (from the edge inward) or `CenterSourced`
(from the centre outward). `Technique` is `Softer` in every surveyed file. The default blend
mode is `Screen`, which lightens; a black glow under `Screen` draws nothing.

> **In Paged:** both glows are drawn; a glow whose `EffectColor` does not resolve is drawn
> white, a shadow black. <SupportBadge feature="effects-transparency.glows" />

## Bevel and emboss

<AttrTable element="BevelAndEmbossSetting" />

Shading that makes the object look raised or sunk. `Style` chooses the kind (`InnerBevel` by
default), `Technique` the profile (`SmoothContour`, `ChiselHard`, `ChiselSoft`), and `Direction`
(`Up` by default) whether it is raised or sunk. The light comes from `Angle` at the height `Altitude`. The facet is `Size`
wide; `Depth`, a percentage that can go far above 100, makes it steeper rather than scaling the
result; `Soften` blurs it. The highlight and shadow each have a colour, an opacity and a blend
mode (`Screen` and `Multiply` by default).

> **In Paged:** bevels are drawn with a facet `Size` wide, shaded against the light. Where the
> facet is wider than half the object, InDesign fades it and Paged does not yet.
> <SupportBadge feature="effects-transparency.bevel-emboss" />

## Satin

<AttrTable element="SatinSetting" />

A soft, inner shading made of two offset copies of the shape: `Angle` and `Distance` set the
offset, `Size` the softness, `InvertEffect` swaps the light and dark areas. Default
`Multiply`, 50 %.

> **In Paged:** satin is drawn from two offset, softened copies of the shape; it is not yet
> matched to InDesign as closely as the other effects. <SupportBadge feature="effects-transparency.satin" />

## Stacking

An outer glow and a drop shadow are drawn beneath the object; the inner effects (inner shadow,
inner glow, bevel, satin) are drawn on top of its fill.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is `Size` a blur radius?**
No. It is the width of the soft band. Outer effects fall off with a standard deviation of
`Size / 2`, inner effects about `0.4 × Size`.

**Which way does a shadow fall for `Angle="120"`?**
Down and to the right. The angle names where the light is, counter-clockwise from the right, and
the shadow falls the opposite way.

**Are `Spread` and `ChokeAmount` in points?**
No. They are percentages of `Size`.

**How is a drop shadow's offset written?**
As `XOffset` and `YOffset` in points, positive right and down.

**Does a frame with no fill cast a shadow?**
Yes, from what it does paint: its stroke band, or, for a text frame, its text.

**How do I tell whether an effect is on?**
A drop shadow is on with `Mode="Drop"`; the other effects here with `Applied="true"`.
InDesign writes the settings even when the effect is off.
