**The composer decides where a paragraph's lines break; the OpenType attributes decide which glyphs a run's characters become.**

**In short:** `Composer` is a paragraph attribute that names the line-breaking engine:
`HL Composer` is the Adobe Paragraph Composer, which weighs all the breaks of a paragraph
together, and `HL Single` is the Adobe Single-line Composer, which sets one line at a time.
On the character side, `Ligatures` switches standard ligatures, `Capitalization` chooses
capitals or small capitals, and a family of `OTF…` attributes switches individual OpenType
features: fractions, ordinals, swashes, discretionary ligatures, slashed zero, titling
alternates, contextual alternates, figure styles and stylistic sets.

## `Composer`

`Composer` is set on a paragraph style or a `<ParagraphStyleRange>`. InDesign writes one of four
values:

| Value | Composer | How it breaks lines |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `HL Composer` | Adobe Paragraph Composer | Considers every possible break in the paragraph and picks the set that gives the most even spacing overall. A change late in a paragraph can move earlier breaks. |
| `HL Single` | Adobe Single-line Composer | Fills one line at a time and never revisits a line once set, the way most word processors break text. |
| `HL Composer Optyca` | Adobe World-Ready Paragraph Composer | Breaks like the Paragraph Composer, and adds shaping for scripts such as Arabic, Hebrew and the Indic scripts. |
| `HL Single Optyca` | Adobe World-Ready Single-line Composer | Breaks like the Single-line Composer, with the same script support. |

The Paragraph Composer is InDesign's default. In the corpus of InDesign-authored documents,
`HL Composer` is by far the most common value and `HL Composer Optyca` appears in a handful;
the census records `Composer` on paragraph styles in 271 documents. A value outside these four
comes from a third-party composer.

> **In Paged:** the Paragraph Composer is a total-fit (Knuth–Plass) line breaker with penalties
> calibrated against InDesign; the Single-line Composer sets one line at a time. The World-Ready
> values compose like their Latin counterparts, and an unknown value composes like the Paragraph
> Composer. The value is kept on save. <SupportBadge feature="typography.knuth-plass" />

## `Ligatures` and `Capitalization`

`Ligatures` (`true` by default) switches the font's standard ligatures, such as "fi" and "fl".
Discretionary ligatures are a separate switch, `OTFDiscretionaryLigature`.

`Capitalization` takes `Normal`, `AllCaps`, `SmallCaps` or `CapToSmallCap`. `AllCaps` displays
the text in capitals; the stored characters keep their case. `SmallCaps` displays lowercase
letters as small capitals using the font's OpenType small capitals; `CapToSmallCap` turns the
capitals into small capitals as well. When a font has no small capitals, InDesign scales
ordinary capitals instead.

## OpenType feature attributes

Each attribute below is a character attribute, set on a run or a character or paragraph style.
The OpenType feature it switches is given for reference.

| Attribute | Type | Feature | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `OTFContextualAlternate` | boolean, default `true` | `calt` | Alternate glyphs chosen by context, as the font designer intended. |
| `OTFDiscretionaryLigature` | boolean | `dlig` | Decorative ligatures such as "ct" and "st". |
| `OTFFraction` | boolean | `frac` | Builds fractions such as 1/2 from digits and a slash. |
| `OTFOrdinal` | boolean | `ordn` | Superior letters for ordinals such as 1st and 2nd. |
| `OTFSwash` | boolean | `swsh` | Swash forms of letters. |
| `OTFTitling` | boolean | `titl` | Capitals designed for large sizes. |
| `OTFSlashedZero` | boolean | `zero` | A zero with a slash, to tell it from the letter O. |
| `OTFFigureStyle` | enumeration | `lnum` / `onum` with `tnum` / `pnum` | Lining or oldstyle digits, tabular or proportional widths. |
| `OTFStylisticSets` | integer | `ss01`–`ss20` | A bit field: bit 0 switches on stylistic set 1, bit 1 set 2, and so on. |

`OTFFigureStyle` takes `Default` (the font's own digits) or one of the four combinations of
tabular or proportional with lining or oldstyle, such as `TabularOldstyle` and
`ProportionalLining`, the values found in the corpus besides `Default`. A feature the font does
not contain has no effect.

InDesign writes further `OTF…` attributes for features with narrower use, such as
`OTFHistorical`, `OTFJustificationAlternate`, `OTFLocale`, `OTFMark`, `OTFStylisticAlternate` and
the CJK `OTFHVKana` and `OTFProportionalMetrics`.

## Frequently asked questions

**What values does the `Composer` attribute take?**
`HL Composer` (Adobe Paragraph Composer), `HL Single` (Adobe Single-line Composer) and their
World-Ready variants `HL Composer Optyca` and `HL Single Optyca`.

**What is the difference between the Paragraph Composer and the Single-line Composer?**
The Paragraph Composer chooses all the line breaks of a paragraph together for the most even
spacing; the Single-line Composer fills one line at a time and does not revisit earlier lines.

**How do I switch on oldstyle figures in IDML?**
Set `OTFFigureStyle` on the run or style, for example to `ProportionalOldstyle` or
`TabularOldstyle`. The font must contain oldstyle figures.

**How are stylistic sets written?**
As one integer, `OTFStylisticSets`, used as a bit field: 1 switches on set 1, 2 set 2, 3 sets 1
and 2, and so on.
