**IDML's typography is a set of attributes on paragraphs and runs: which face, what size and spacing, which OpenType features, how lines break, and what happens around them.**

**In short:** Every typographic decision in an IDML story is an attribute on a
`<ParagraphStyleRange>`, a `<CharacterStyleRange>` or one of the styles they apply. Character
attributes pick the face, size, tracking, kerning and OpenType features of a run. Paragraph
attributes pick the alignment, the composer that breaks lines, hyphenation, drop caps, tab
stops, lists, keep options and column spans. This chapter explains what each family means and
how InDesign spells it; each page notes in an aside how far Paged honours it.

## Where typography is set

[The paragraph model](/docs/idml/stories-text/the-paragraph-model) covers the shape of a story:
flat paragraph and character ranges over a stream of characters. Typography rides on those
ranges and on the styles they apply, resolved in the order described under
[conflict resolution](/docs/idml/styles/conflict-resolution). Three characters inside `Content`
also matter here: the tab (U+0009), which moves the text after it to the next tab stop; the
forced line break (U+2028), which ends a line without ending the paragraph; and the paragraph
mark `<Br/>`.

## In this chapter

- **[Fonts and faces](/docs/idml/typography/fonts-and-faces)** — `AppliedFont` names the family,
  `FontStyle` the face; `Fonts.xml` lists the fonts a document uses but contains none.
- **[Spacing and leading](/docs/idml/typography/spacing-and-leading)** — `Tracking`, kerning
  (`KerningMethod`, `KerningValue`), `Leading` and auto leading, baseline shift, scaling and skew.
- **[Composer and OpenType features](/docs/idml/typography/composer-and-opentype)** — the
  `Composer` that breaks a paragraph into lines, `Ligatures`, `Capitalization` and the `OTF…`
  feature attributes.
- **[Justification and line-breaking](/docs/idml/typography/justification-and-linebreaking)** —
  the `Justification` values, word and letter spacing, glyph scaling and hyphenation.
- **[Drop caps and tabs](/docs/idml/typography/drop-caps-and-tabs)** — `DropCapCharacters` and
  `DropCapLines`, and the `<TabList>` of tab stops the tab character moves to.
- **[Lists, keep options and span columns](/docs/idml/typography/lists-keep-span)** — bullets and
  numbering, the keep options that control where paragraphs break between frames, and
  paragraphs that span or split columns.

The pages follow the order a reader meets these questions when setting a paragraph: the face,
the spacing within a line, the breaking into lines, then the paragraph-level features.

## Frequently asked questions

**Where are typographic attributes stored in IDML?**
On the `<ParagraphStyleRange>` and `<CharacterStyleRange>` elements of a story and on the
paragraph and character styles they apply. A few are typed `<Properties>` children rather than
attributes: `AppliedFont`, `Leading`, `TabList` and some list settings.

**Does an IDML file contain fonts?**
No. `Resources/Fonts.xml` lists the fonts the document uses, with their names and status, but no
font data. See [fonts and faces](/docs/idml/typography/fonts-and-faces).

**How is a tab written in IDML?**
As the tab character U+0009 inside `<Content>`, often written `&#9;`. The tab stops it moves to
are records in the paragraph's `<TabList>`; see
[drop caps and tabs](/docs/idml/typography/drop-caps-and-tabs).
