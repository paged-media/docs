**An item shows when its layer is visible and the item itself is visible; it prints when, in addition, its layer is printable and the item is not marked non-printing.**

**In short:** Four switches decide whether a page item appears. On the layer: `Visible`
(hidden layers show nothing) and `Printable` (a non-printing layer shows on screen but not in
print or PDF). On the item: `Visible` (hides that item alone) and `Nonprinting` (shows on screen
only). `Locked`, on the layer or the item, only stops editing. All of them default to showing
and printing.

## The layer's switches

- **`Visible="false"`**: the layer is hidden. None of its items are drawn, on screen or in
  output.
- **`Printable="false"`**: the layer is a non-printing layer. Its items show while editing but
  are left out of print and export.
- **`Locked="true"`**: its items cannot be selected or moved. It changes nothing about what is
  drawn.

All three default to the showing state: visible, printable, unlocked. For a group, the switches
of the group's layer apply to all its members.

## The item's own switches

- **`Visible="false"`** on a page item hides that item only, whatever its layer. In InDesign it
  is the eye on the object's row in the Layers panel.
- **`Nonprinting="true"`** keeps the item on screen and out of print and export, like a
  non-printing layer for one item.
- **`Locked="true"`** blocks selection, like a locked layer.

## Nested layers

If a layer is written inside another `<Layer>`, it inherits its parent's state: a visible
layer inside a hidden parent is hidden, and a layer inside a locked parent is locked. See
[the layer model](/docs/idml/layers/the-layer-model).

> **In Paged:** the renderer draws an item only when its layer is visible, its layer is
> printable and the item is visible; a nested layer inherits a hidden or locked parent. The
> renderer makes no difference between screen and print, so a non-printing layer is hidden in
> the rendered page too. Locked layers and items are drawn and only block selection in the
> editor. <SupportBadge feature="layers.model" /> <SupportBadge feature="layers.ops" />

## When content goes missing

1. Read the item's `ItemLayer`, or its group's.
2. Find that `<Layer>` in `designmap.xml`, and any `<Layer>` it is nested in.
3. Check `Visible` and `Printable` there.
4. Check the item's own `Visible` and `Nonprinting`.

If all of these show the item, the cause is elsewhere: geometry (off the page, on the
pasteboard), paint (`Swatch/None`), or text that is overset.

## Frequently asked questions

**When is a page item drawn?**
When its layer is visible and the item's own `Visible` is not `false`. For printing and export,
its layer must also be printable and the item must not be `Nonprinting`.

**Does Printable="false" hide items?**
Not while editing in InDesign: the layer shows on screen and is left out of print and PDF.
A renderer that only produces output treats it like a hidden layer.

**Does a Locked layer change the output?**
No. Locking only stops items from being selected or moved.

**Can one item be hidden without hiding its layer?**
Yes, with `Visible="false"` on the item.
