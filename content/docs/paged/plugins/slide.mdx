**In short:** `paged.slide` makes presentations a document type. A PowerPoint
`.pptx` opens as native pages: one slide per page, layouts as master spreads,
shapes, charts, tables and SmartArt as editable native content, and speaker notes
kept with each slide. PowerPoint itself is the reference: geometry, baselines,
chart bars and table styles are checked against what it draws. It is built into
the editor (the built-in modules are not published to npm), and parts of it are
still in development.

## What it does

- Reads `.pptx`, `.ppsx` and `.potx`: masters, layouts, themes, every preset and
  custom shape, text, pictures, charts, tables (including PowerPoint's built-in
  table styles) and SmartArt drawings.
- Resolves PowerPoint's inheritance (theme, master, layout, slide) and writes the
  deck as a native document, a page per slide.
- A slide sorter (reorder, duplicate, delete, hide) and a speaker-notes panel; a
  new slide from a layout. Slides, layouts and placeholders are objects that
  scripts and panels read and write (notes, the hidden flag, the transition).
- Fonts the deck embeds are used when their licence allows installing or
  editing them; restricted and preview-only faces are named, not used.
- Export back to `.pptx` from the original deck: the page order, duplicated and
  deleted slides, hidden flags, speaker notes and sections, checked by
  PowerPoint opening the written deck without a repair prompt. An unedited
  document exports the original bytes.
- In development: writing edited slide content into the exported deck, and a
  slideshow with a presenter view.

## How it's built

A Cargo workspace (`pptx-core`, `pptx-import`, `pptx-export`, `pptx-fonts`,
`slide-resolve`, `slide-geom`, `slide-chart`, `slide-native`, `slide-js`) over the
shared OOXML package layer (`paged-ooxml`, in `platform/shared/ooxml`),
plus a TS bundle (`@paged-media/slide`). Scripts drive PowerPoint to record the
answers the tests compare against.
