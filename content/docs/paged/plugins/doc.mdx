**In short:** `paged.doc` brings Word documents into paged. A `.docx` is read into
native content (paragraph and character styles, lists, tables, images, links,
notes) and either poured into a frame of a layout or opened as a document of its
own. Saving back writes a targeted patch into the original file, so whatever paged
did not touch stays exactly as Word wrote it.

## What it does

- Reads the body, styles, lists, tables, images, hyperlinks and notes of a
  `.docx` or `.dotx`, preserving every part it does not model verbatim.
- Places a Word document into a frame as one undoable step, with an outline and
  styles panel over the imported structure.
- Saves edits back as byte-level patches (text, paragraph properties, table rows
  and columns); edits it cannot write are listed in the Problems panel rather than
  dropped silently.
- In development: opening a Word document on its own, a page per section, with
  Word's own pagination (line spacing, breaks and keeps).

## How it's built

A Cargo workspace over a shared OOXML package layer (`paged-ooxml`) that lowers
DOCX into native content, plus a TS bundle (`@paged-media/doc`). An identity-based
alignment between the source and the edited document is what lets save-back patch
only what changed.
