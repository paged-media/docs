**In short:** `paged.publish` is how foreign formats enter and leave paged. Its
IDML importer and exporter carry InDesign packages in and out, and an unchanged
package comes back identical to the byte. Its PDF plugin turns a PDF into a native
document: pages, vectors with their colours mapped to swatches, images, and (in
development) text rebuilt into editable paragraphs.

## What it does

- IDML import through the engine's native-document door, and IDML export, as
  typed commands scripts can call.
- A corpus sweep that checks every package in the test corpus survives a round
  trip unchanged.
- PDF reading with PDFium: vectors and colours become native shapes and
  swatches, and images decode whatever their filter chain.
- In development: opening a PDF in the editor end to end, and rebuilding its
  positioned glyphs into paragraphs of styled runs for editing.

## How it's built

The `idml-import` and `idml-export` crates and the `pdf-import` crate, plus two TS
bundles (`@paged-media/publish`, `@paged-media/pdf`).
