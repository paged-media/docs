**In short:** `paged.sheet` is a full spreadsheet engine in Rust/WASM that lives
inside a print layout — a dependency-graph calc engine with dynamic-array spill,
structured tables, a chart engine, and preservation-safe XLSX round-trip. It
lowers to native `<Table>` content and is editable in-frame.

## What it does

- Calc engine (dependency graph, dirty recalc, Excel-compatible numerics) with
  <Fact id="sheet.functions" /> registered functions and dynamic-array spill.
- Structured tables, a chart engine (plotters → a custom drawing backend →
  frozen chart geometry), and number formatting with locales.
- XLSX import/export with a byte-faithful preservation invariant.
- Page-surface lowering to native tables, multi-frame pagination, and an in-frame
  grid with a modal edit session; consumes governed datasets from
  [paged.data](/docs/paged/plugins/data).
- Spreadsheet editing in the grid: range selection, the grid keys, find,
  formula-carrying copy and paste, the fill handle, a formula bar that completes
  function names from the engine, and host undo outside the edit session.
- Placing on the page as typed commands: a range, a defined name or a sheet's
  print area (several ranges included), in one frame with its view (window,
  fixed or fit sizing, top-left cell, printed headings and gridlines) or
  paginated across a chain of frames; deleting a chart removes it and its placed
  copies, and only its parts from the saved `.xlsx`
  ([paged.sheet commands](/docs/paged/commands/sheet)).
- Merging cells, borders, column widths and row heights, and CSV import, as
  typed commands too.
- In development: cell styles, freeze panes, and the rest of the workbook as
  typed objects for scripts and bindings (the object model).

## How it's built

A Cargo workspace (`sheet-core`, `sheet-parser`, `sheet-calc`, `sheet-fn`,
`sheet-format`, `sheet-xlsx`, `sheet-lower`, `sheet-grid`, `sheet-chart`, `sheet-js`) plus a TS
bundle; a coverage gate requires every function to have a test.
