**What open-source desktop publishing software is there, and how do you choose?**

**In short:** the two main open-source desktop publishing tools are **Scribus**
(mature, GPL, strong on print) and **Paged** (open source, IDML-native,
scriptable, web-delivered). **DesignCraft**, part of the ArtCraft suite, is a
newer, pre-alpha MIT/Apache-licensed take on the InDesign workflow. If you also count "free but proprietary," **Affinity**
is free of charge yet closed. For an open *format* that round-trips with the
InDesign ecosystem plus scripting and plugins, Paged is the distinctive option;
for proven print output today, Scribus leads. *(Snapshot: 2026-06-23; DesignCraft added 2026-10-12.)*

## The open options

- **[Scribus](/docs/paged/compare/paged-vs-scribus)** — the long-standing
  open-source (GPL) DTP application. Mature layout and excellent CMYK/spot color
  and print production. Desktop-only; partial IDML import; no plugin platform.
- **[Paged](/docs/paged/)** — open source (MPL-2.0 OR PMEL), built on a valid
  IDML container, with a [scripting layer](/docs/paged/scripting) and a [plugin
  SDK](/docs/paged/plugin-sdk). Web/WebGPU and headless delivery. Layout and print
  depth are emerging.
- **[DesignCraft (ArtCraft)](/docs/paged/compare/paged-vs-artcraft)** — a
  clean-room, MIT OR Apache-2.0 reimplementation of the InDesign workflow in Rust,
  one of twelve [ArtCraft](https://getartcraft.com/apps) apps. Desktop and web,
  IDML import and export, PDF/X-4 export, and agent control over MCP. Pre-alpha:
  threading text by hand is still missing, and there is no plugin platform.

## "Free" that isn't open

- **[Affinity (by Canva)](/docs/paged/compare/paged-vs-affinity)** — free of
  charge but proprietary: a closed `.af` format, no scripting, no plugins, and
  IDML import without export. Free is not the same as open.

## How to choose

- Need **proven print output** today? Scribus.
- Need an **open format that round-trips with InDesign**, plus **scripting and
  plugins** and **automation**? Paged.
- Want a **permissively licensed, InDesign-like desktop app** and can wait for
  it to mature? Watch DesignCraft.
- Want a **free, polished single app** and don't need openness or scripting?
  Affinity.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the best open-source alternative to InDesign?**
Scribus is the most mature open-source DTP app. Paged is the open option that is
IDML-compatible (so it round-trips with InDesign) and adds scripting, plugins,
and automation — at the cost of still-emerging layout depth.

**Is there free desktop publishing software?**
Yes — Scribus, Paged and DesignCraft are free and open source; Affinity is free of charge but
proprietary.

**Can open-source DTP tools read InDesign files?**
Via IDML, InDesign's interchange format. Paged reads and writes IDML natively;
DesignCraft imports and exports it (not yet measured on real InDesign files);
Scribus support is partial.
